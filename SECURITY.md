## Reporting a Vulnerability

The Marko team takes the security of our application and users seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, please bring it to our attention responsibly.

**Please do NOT file a public issue on GitHub for security vulnerabilities.**

### How to Report Privately:
1. **GitHub Private Security Advisory**: Go to the **Security** tab of the repository and click **"Report a vulnerability"**.
2. **Direct Email**: You can also email the maintainer directly at **contact.arunjay@gmail.com** with the subject line:
   `[SECURITY] Vulnerability in Marko - <Short Description>`.

### Information to Include in Your Report:
- A clear description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce the issue (proof-of-concept script, video, or markdown snippet).
- Potential impact and affected platforms (Windows Electron, Web, PWA, Android, iOS).
- Any proposed fixes or mitigations (optional).

### What Happens Next?
- We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 hours.
- We will evaluate the report, verify the issue, and provide an estimated timeline for a patch.
- Once a fix is verified and deployed, we will publish a release note and credit your contribution (unless you prefer to remain anonymous).
